# Philippine ecommerce listings

Turn supplied product facts into clear listings for shoppers in the Philippines. Follow the seller's brand voice and requested language. Use the target platform's field names or limits only when the user provides them or they have been verified for that platform.

## Source and claims

- Work from confirmed product name, brand, material, size, color, variants, contents, price, condition, availability, and seller-provided shipping or warranty terms.
- Distinguish a product fact from a marketing claim. Do not invent certifications, health or safety benefits, “original” status, ratings, scarcity, delivery times, returns, or platform compliance.
- Use Philippine search terms naturally. Do not stuff keywords or add unrelated marketplace names.
- Keep variant choices and prices aligned with the provided data. If a key specification is missing, leave it out of the public copy and list it for verification.

## Output

Provide a concise title, 3–5 factual highlights, a scannable description, and a variants/contents section when applicable. Put unresolved facts under “Verify before publishing.” Format confirmed PHP prices with a peso symbol; do not calculate tax, discounts, or shipping charges unless the figures and rule are supplied. The output is a draft, not a published listing.
